# DividendOS 1.10

Dividend receipt entry is available directly from the selected holding's portfolio overview. The existing form, validation and durable save path are reused. Income summary rows align their labels and amounts; duplicate trailing-year totals and cash-funding warnings move to the relevant expanded analysis/recovery sections. A paused cash balance identifies its actual blockers without changing funding safeguards.

The economic P/L formula remains unchanged. ROC-adjusted costs remain available in a collapsed section only when an actual basis adjustment exists. Completed calendar months are used for the three-month average; growth labels identify increase/decrease and both exact periods, and insufficient recorded history suppresses the comparison. Historical record completeness is not inferred.

Composition graphs include single holdings at 100%, display cents, and normalize sub-dollar totals against their actual sum. Calendar-year totals state the exact year. The app has no schema or cloud conflict-resolution changes. Synthetic QA never writes to user cloud accounts.

Validation and artifact fingerprints are recorded in docs/releases/1.10.0.publication.json. Physical Galaxy/Toss checks remain pending after the signed in-app update and are not a publication gate.
